Two problems, one marketplace. University students apply to dozens of postings with almost no signal about which ones actually fit them. Mid-tier Fortune 500 employers — large, well-known companies that aren't Google or Goldman — struggle to compete for student attention on campus. Ambees connects the two sides directly.

The core idea is AI cross-matching: instead of treating an application as a yes/no for a single job, Ambees reads a candidate's profile and routes them toward better-fit roles across an employer's openings, including ones they wouldn't have thought to apply to. Recruiters get AI-generated candidate summaries instead of stacks of resumes; students get a swipe-style experience with voice-agent onboarding. I founded Ambees as a Delaware C-corp and lead it as CEO, with Yiran Hu as CTO. We're backed by Georgetown's Summer Launch Incubator and accepted into Microsoft for Startups. Better matching, not more applications.
